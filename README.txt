THE CURSED GARAGE
Live game: https://wikky1984.github.io/halloween2026/

Files
- index.html: game screens
- style.css: responsive design and reduced-motion support
- game.js: game logic and optional synthesized audio
- haunted-garage.wav: original looping background music (12 seconds, mono)
- icon.svg: favicon

Operation
The game is hosted by GitHub Pages from the main branch and repository root. Visitors need no installation, login, or personal information. Sound is ON by default but begins only when the visitor taps ENTER, as mobile browsers require. The SOUND button turns it off at any point. Original looped background music plays during the challenge; heartbeats and other effects are synthesized in the browser at modest levels. In the final challenge, an object falls toward the ring. Tap during the steady green hit window. Three hits win; three early, late, or unanswered notes lose. The six main endings vary by chosen object and win/loss, with random spooky titles. With reduced motion enabled, the note stays still and appears in the hit zone during the timing window. The coffin, pumpkin, old door, ghost, icon, garage scene, poster graphics, music, and sound effects were created for this project. The character art is lightweight local SVG. There are no stock assets, external assets, or analytics, and there are no strobes or gore.

If the game does not load, check GitHub Settings > Pages for a successful deployment, then confirm index.html, style.css, game.js, and icon.svg are in the root of main. If audio does not play, confirm SOUND ON, tap ENTER, and check the phone media volume and silent mode. Tapping SOUND OFF and then SOUND ON plays a short test tone. If the QR is difficult to scan outdoors, add steady light to the poster without glare and test from several distances.

Before Halloween, scan the printed QR with an iPhone and an Android phone. Play all three choices, both winning and losing, with sound on and off, and check the result and replay buttons.
